## ⚠️ Commonly confused facts

| Don't mix up | The difference |
|---|---|
| **UK vs Great Britain** | UK includes Northern Ireland; GB does not |
| **Crown dependencies vs overseas territories** | Isle of Man & Channel Islands vs Falklands & St Helena. Neither is part of the UK |
| **Julius Caesar vs Claudius** | Caesar failed (55 BC); Claudius succeeded (AD 43) |
| **St Augustine vs St Columba vs St Patrick** | Canterbury vs Iona vs Ireland |
| **Bannockburn vs Bosworth** | 1314 Scots beat English vs 1485 Tudors win the Wars of the Roses |
| **Agincourt vs Trafalgar vs Waterloo** | Henry V 1415 vs Nelson 1805 (sea) vs Wellington 1815 |
| **Magna Carta vs Bill of Rights vs Habeas Corpus** | 1215 king under law vs 1689 Parliament's power vs 1679 no unlawful imprisonment |
| **1707 vs 1801** | Act of Union with Scotland vs union with Ireland |
| **1807 vs 1833** | Slave **trade** abolished vs **slavery** abolished |
| **1918 vs 1928** | Women over 30 vote vs women vote at 21 |
| **Lancaster vs York** | Red rose vs white rose |
| **Cavaliers vs Roundheads** | King vs Parliament |
| **Whigs vs Tories** | Tories = Conservatives today |
| **Church of England vs Church of Scotland** | Anglican, monarch is head, Archbishop of Canterbury vs Presbyterian, Moderator (1 year) |
| **Wales & NI churches** | No established church |
| **Magistrates (E&W) vs NI** | Unpaid volunteers vs paid, legally qualified District Judge |
| **Crown Court vs Sheriff Court** | Serious crimes in E, W, NI vs Scotland |
| **Jury 12 vs 15** | England, Wales, NI vs Scotland |
| **Youth Court vs Children's Hearings** | E, W, NI vs Scotland |
| **Council of Europe vs EU** | Different organisations. Council of Europe made the ECHR and can't make laws |
| **Commonwealth vs Commonwealth (1649)** | Group of nations today vs Cromwell's republic |
| **Christmas vs Boxing Day** | 25 vs 26 December |
| **Shrove Tuesday vs Ash Wednesday** | Day **before** Lent (pancakes) vs **first day** of Lent |
| **Good Friday vs Easter Sunday** | Jesus died vs rose from the dead |
| **Diwali vs Vaisakhi** | Hindus & Sikhs, Festival of Lights vs Sikhs only, 14 April, Khalsa |
| **Eid al-Fitr vs Eid ul Adha** | End of Ramadan vs Ibrahim's sacrifice |
| **Halloween vs Bonfire Night** | 31 Oct, pagan roots vs 5 Nov, Guy Fawkes |
| **Hogmanay** | Scottish New Year's Eve (31 Dec) |
| **Ben Nevis vs Snowdon** | Highest in UK (Scotland) vs highest in Wales |
| **Windermere vs Loch Lomond** | Largest lake in England vs largest fresh water in mainland Britain |
| **Grand National vs Royal Ascot** | Aintree (Liverpool) vs Berkshire |
| **Chichester vs Knox-Johnston** | First solo round the world vs first solo **non-stop** |
| **Turner vs Constable** | Modern landscapes vs Dedham Vale |
| **Elgar vs Holst vs Britten** | Pomp and Circumstance vs The Planets vs Peter Grimes / Aldeburgh |
| **Big Ben** | The **bell**, not the tower (Elizabeth Tower) |
