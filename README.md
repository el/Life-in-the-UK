## ⚡ Quick facts about the test

- **24** multiple-choice questions
- **45** minutes
- Pass mark: **75%**, which means **18 of 24** correct
- Fee: **£50**
- Book online at gov.uk at least **3 days** ahead
- Questions come from **chapters 1 to 5** of the official handbook

---

## 🧭 How to use this guide

1. Read the chapters in order (1 to 5). Chapter 3 (History) is the longest and carries the most questions.
2. After each chapter, test yourself with the matching section of the [practice questions](07-practice-questions.md).
3. In the last few days, revise from the [cheat sheet](06-cheat-sheet.md).
4. Aim to score **90%+** on practice tests before you book the real one.

## ⚠️ A note on outdated facts

The official handbook was published in 2013 and some facts have since changed.
The test is written from the handbook, so **learn the handbook version and know the update**.

| Handbook says | Current reality |
|---|---|
| Queen Elizabeth II is monarch | **King Charles III** since 8 September 2022 |
| National anthem "God Save the Queen" | "God Save the **King**" |
| UK is a member of the EU | UK **left the EU on 31 January 2020** |
| National Assembly for Wales, 60 AMs | Renamed **Senedd Cymru / Welsh Parliament** (2020); members are MSs; increased to 96 members from 2026 |
| NI Assembly has 108 MLAs | Reduced to **90** MLAs (2017) |
| Commonwealth has 54 members | **56** members (Gabon and Togo joined 2022) |
| Council of Europe has 47 members | **46** (Russia expelled 2022) |
| Small claims limit £5,000 (England & Wales) | **£10,000** |
| National Lottery minimum age 16 | **18** (since 2021) |
| Independent Police Complaints Commission (IPCC) | Replaced by the **IOPC** (2018) |
| Snowdonia | Now usually called **Eryri** |
| Voting needs no ID | **Photo ID** now needed at polling stations in Great Britain (since 2023) |

Each chapter flags these with a ⚠️ symbol.
