## Section A: Values and the UK

**1.** Which of these is a fundamental principle of British life?
- A) Extremism
- B) Individual liberty
- C) Political censorship
- D) A single state religion

**2.** Which TWO are responsibilities of people living in the UK?
- A) Respect and obey the law
- B) Vote for the governing party
- C) Look after the area in which you live
- D) Attend church

**3.** True or false? Great Britain includes Northern Ireland.

**4.** Which of these is a Crown dependency?
- A) The Falkland Islands
- B) The Isle of Man
- C) St Helena
- D) Gibraltar

**5.** What is the full official name of the UK?
- A) The United Kingdom of England and Ireland
- B) Great Britain and the Islands
- C) The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland
- D) The British Isles

**6.** Which country's flag is NOT part of the Union Flag?
- A) England
- B) Scotland
- C) Ireland
- D) Wales

**7.** What does the cross of St Andrew look like?
- A) Red cross on a white background
- B) Diagonal white cross on a blue background
- C) Diagonal red cross on a white background
- D) Red dragon on white and green

**8.** True or false? The Channel Islands are part of the UK.

**9.** At a citizenship ceremony, new citizens make an oath or affirmation and a…
- A) Speech
- B) Pledge
- C) Payment
- D) Vote

**10.** Which is a freedom offered by the UK?
- A) Freedom from paying taxes
- B) Freedom from unfair discrimination
- C) Freedom to ignore the law
- D) Freedom from jury service

<details>
<summary><b>Show answers (Section A)</b></summary>

1. **B**: Individual liberty
2. **A and C**
3. **False**: Great Britain is England, Scotland and Wales only
4. **B**: The Isle of Man
5. **C**
6. **D**: Wales
7. **B**
8. **False**: they are Crown dependencies
9. **B**: Pledge
10. **B**

</details>
