# 2. What is the UK?

[← Back to contents](README.md)

---

## The names: get these right

| Name | Includes |
|---|---|
| **United Kingdom (UK)** | England, Scotland, Wales **and Northern Ireland** |
| **Great Britain** | England, Scotland and Wales **only** |
| **British Isles** | The whole group of islands, including the Republic of Ireland (a separate country) |

- Full official name: **The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland**.
- "Britain" is often used to mean the whole UK, but it is not the official term.

---

## The four nations

| Nation | Capital | Patron saint | Saint's day | National flower |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| England | London | St George | 23 April | Rose |
| Scotland | Edinburgh | St Andrew | 30 November | Thistle |
| Wales | Cardiff | St David | 1 March | Daffodil |
| Northern Ireland | Belfast | St Patrick | 17 March | Shamrock |

- **London** is also the capital of the whole UK.
- Only **Scotland** (St Andrew's Day) and **Northern Ireland** (St Patrick's Day) have their saint's day as an official holiday.

---

## Not part of the UK

### Crown dependencies

- **Isle of Man**
- **Channel Islands** (e.g. Jersey and Guernsey)

Facts:

- They have their **own governments**.
- They are **closely linked** to the UK but are **not part of it**.

### British overseas territories

- Examples: **St Helena**, the **Falkland Islands**.
- Linked to the UK, but **not part of it**.

---

## How the UK is governed (overview)

- The UK is governed by **Parliament at Westminster** in London.
- **Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland** also have their own parliament or assembly (**devolved** government) with powers in some areas.
- See [chapter 5](05-government-and-law.md) for detail.

---

## The Union Flag (Union Jack)

Made of three crosses:

| Cross | Country | Look |
|---|---|---|
| St George | England | Red cross on a white background |
| St Andrew | Scotland | Diagonal white cross on a blue background |
| St Patrick | Ireland | Diagonal red cross on a white background |

- **Wales is not on the Union Flag.** Wales was already united with England when the first Union Flag was made (1606).
- The **Welsh flag** has a **red dragon** on a **white and green** background.
- The current Union Flag dates from **1801**, when Ireland joined.
