# 0. About the Test

[← Back to contents](README.md)

---

## Who needs to take it

- People applying for **settlement** (Indefinite Leave to Remain) or **British citizenship**.
- Usually required for applicants aged **18 to 64**.
- People under 18 or aged 65+ are normally exempt.
- You also need to prove your **English** (normally at **B1** level or above on the CEFR scale, speaking and listening).

---

## The format

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Number of questions | 24 |
| Time allowed | 45 minutes |
| Pass mark | 75% (18 correct answers) |
| Cost | £50 |
| Language | English (some Welsh or Scottish Gaelic options exist) |
| Where | Approved test centres only (not online) |
| Results | Given on the day, before you leave |
| Retakes | Allowed. Wait at least **7 days**. No limit on attempts. Pay each time. |

### Question types

1. **Single answer**: pick 1 correct option out of 4.
2. **Two answers**: pick the 2 correct options out of 4.
3. **True or false**: decide whether one statement is true.
4. **Which is correct?**: pick the true statement out of 2.

---

## Booking

- Book online on the official gov.uk website.
- Book at least **3 days** in advance.
- Use the **same ID** for booking and on the day (e.g. passport, biometric residence permit).
- Bring the ID with you. No ID means you cannot sit the test and you lose the fee.

---

## What is (and isn't) tested

- ✅ Chapters 1 to 5 of the official handbook.
- ❌ Not tested: the introduction, glossary, or "check that you understand" boxes on their own.
- History (chapter 3) and Society (chapter 4) produce the most questions.
- Many questions ask about **dates**, **people**, and **numbers**. Use the [cheat sheet](06-cheat-sheet.md).

---

## Suggested 3-week study plan

| Week | Days | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–2 | Chapters 1 and 2 |
| 1 | 3–7 | Chapter 3 (History). Split into: Early Britain, Middle Ages, Tudors & Stuarts, 1700s–1800s, 1900s–today |
| 2 | 1–4 | Chapter 4 (Society, festivals, sport, arts) |
| 2 | 5–7 | Chapter 5 (Government and law) |
| 3 | 1–5 | Practice questions daily. Re-read the weak areas. |
| 3 | 6–7 | Cheat sheet only. Rest before the test. |

---

## Study tips

- 🗓️ Make **flashcards** for dates, people and their achievements.
- 🔁 Revisit a section after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days (spaced repetition).
- 🎯 Watch for **"which is NOT…"** and **"choose TWO"** questions.
- ⚖️ Learn the **differences between the four nations** (courts, churches, saints' days, parliaments). These are favourite trick questions.
- 🔢 Learn **numbers** exactly: 650 MPs, 129 MSPs, jury of 12 or 15, etc.

---

## Test-day tips

- Arrive early. Bring your ID.
- You can usually do a short practice question first.
- Read every option before answering.
- Flag hard questions and come back. You can review answers before submitting.
- 45 minutes for 24 questions is almost 2 minutes each. There is plenty of time.
